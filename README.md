>Pictured Above: Intensity of the Diffraction Grating of a grid of circular apertures in the Far-Field (Fraunhofer) Limit (Made with Python). Because EM fields oscillate too rapidly for even the most sensitive equipment to detect directly, the intensity is the physical quantity we measure. 


**Overview:** This repository houses some of my work done for my PH353 Optics & Waves class and lab. 

**Plots:** I've written a couple plots (mostly **python**, some in **MATLAB**) to model certain problems/scenarios, such as:
- The intensity of diffraction gratings of various geometries
- A comparison of a Fourier series to its original function as the number of terms increases
- The output intensity of an interferometric gravimeter (a Michelson interferometer with one of the mirrors moving under the influence of gravity).
These can be found in the plots folder
